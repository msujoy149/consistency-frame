# Consistency Frame — One Character, Every Scene

Original explainer script for the P1-006 reference production.
Narrator-only. No character is named, depicted, or registered.
All six lines are spoken by one narrator voice (en-US).

## Narration lines

- **l1** (shot s1): One character. Every scene. Exactly the same face, every time.
- **l2** (shot s2): Most tools redraw the character in every shot. The eyes drift, the hair changes, and the story breaks.
- **l3** (shot s3): Consistency Frame locks the design first: face, proportions, colours, and voice.
- **l4** (shot s4): Every shot is checked against that one approved reference, before it is ever rendered.
- **l5** (shot s5): The same character, the same voice. Scene after scene, episode after episode.
- **l6** (shot s6): One character. One voice. Every scene. That is Consistency Frame.

## Timing

- One short pause (0.5 s) follows each line except the last.
- 1.0 s of music lead-in before l1; 1.5 s of music tail-out after l6.
- Target total duration: 30–60 s (the pipeline measures the real
  narration durations and the QC stage verifies the final total).
