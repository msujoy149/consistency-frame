## Narration lines

- **l1** (shot s1): Before the sun wakes the river, Nadia lights the first lantern of the day.
- **l2** (shot s2): She walks the old stone steps down to the water, carrying the light.
- **l3** (shot s3a): At the river's edge, she sets the lantern on the water and lets it go.
- **l4** (shot s3b): The lantern drifts away, and the whole morning holds its breath.
- **l5** (shot s4): In the market, she gathers marigolds bright as the evening sun.
- **l6** (shot s5): Rain comes without asking, and she opens her umbrella over the day.
- **l7** (shot s6a): At dusk, she lifts the sky lantern high, its flame reaching for the dark.
- **l8** (shot s6b): She lets it go, and the river light rises to meet the first stars.
