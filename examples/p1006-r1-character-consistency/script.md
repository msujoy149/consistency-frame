# Mara and the Compass Garden

Original short story for the P1-006-R1 character-consistency
production. Narrator-only. One fictional, project-local adult
character (Mara, DEMO-CHAR-001, age 28). All six lines are spoken
by one narrator voice (en-US). No canonical user character is
named, depicted, or registered.

## Narration lines

- **l1** (shot s1): Mara drew maps of places no one had ever seen — until her grandmother's compass pointed somewhere new.
- **l2** (shot s2): It did not point north. It pointed through the old forest, past the blank edge of every map.
- **l3** (shot s3): She had drawn a hundred imaginary worlds. She had never once stepped inside one.
- **l4** (shot s4): The compass grew warm, and the map redrew itself — a stream, a stone, a door of flowers.
- **l5** (shot s5): So Mara ran — past the meadow, past the last fence, past every reason to turn back.
- **l6** (shot s6): And there it was: the garden from her grandmother's stories. One compass. One mapmaker. Every scene, the same Mara.
