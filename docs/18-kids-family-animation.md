## Character performance and story

Personality decides movement; eyes lead the audience; every important movement has a reason. Ten reusable story templates for original scripts: funny animal stories, cute animal adventures, friendship stories, family stories, mystery and surprise, cartoon vehicle adventures, fantasy adventures, moral stories, funny character interactions, educational entertainment.

## Voice, sound, and short-form structure

Natural human delivery, mouth matched to sounds, listener reacts, only the intended speaker's mouth moves (see the `voice-consistency`, `language-authenticity`, and `emotional-performance` skills). A shot-level sound plan: dialogue, narration, foley, animal and vehicle sounds, ambience, music, action effects, transitions, emotional cues, audio perspective, lip-sync timing. Short-form structure: opening visual, first-second hook, setup, character introduction, main action, escalation, surprise or emotional payoff, ending, replay potential — understandable without sound wherever practical, no slow intros or logos.

## Script-only workflow

The user gives a script (plus optional character references and special instructions); the system reads it, identifies the category, loads this skill and the supporting skills, prepares a storyboard and shot list, selects camera angles from the story, creates the assets, generates and assembles the video, runs QC, and delivers the finished video with a concise production summary. The fifteen steps are enumerated in the skill (§15). If the script lacks an essential detail, ask the smallest necessary clarification.
