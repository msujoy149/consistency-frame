# Kids Animation — Permanent Reference Library

The permanent reference library for the `kids-family-animation` skill: verified production knowledge, not just links. When the user supplies a script, the system consults this library and decides visual style, animation method, camera angles, shot sizes, character actions, dialogue style, sound design, scene timing, and editing structure automatically.

## Structure — ten knowledge areas

Every discovery is classified into exactly one of these ten areas:

1. **Camera and cinematography** — angles, shot sizes, movement, lens and perspective, composition, and the camera decision system. Full reference: `kids-animation-camera-language.md`.
2. **3D CGI production** — modelling, rigging, animation principles, lighting and rendering, environment and scene construction, and the AI reproduction strategy.
3. **2D animation** — line weight, shape language, colour palette, background art, posing, expressions, limited animation, frame-by-frame movement, motion cycles, smear frames, timing, transitions, pans and zooms, metaphors, parallax, compositing, anime-specific framing.
4. **AI animation production** — consistent references, multi-view sheets, image-to-video, controlled camera, first/last-frame conditioning, motion references, scene continuation, repair, multi-clip continuity.
5. **Character performance** — personality, facial reactions, eye direction, body language, comedy timing, surprise, suspense, curiosity, friendship, conflict, emotional development, interaction, physical comedy, visual storytelling.
6. **Storytelling** — the ten story structures (funny animal, cute animal adventures, friendship, family, mystery and surprise, cartoon vehicle adventures, fantasy adventures, moral stories, funny character interactions, educational entertainment) and how each one is built.
7. **Dialogue and voice** — natural delivery, lip-sync, listener reactions, single-speaker mouth rule, breathing and pauses, per-character language and voice.
8. **Audio and sound design** — shot-level sound plans, dialogue, narration, foley, animal and vehicle sounds, ambience, music, action effects, transitions, emotional cues, audio perspective, lip-sync timing.
9. **Editing and retention** — short-form structure, first-second hook, escalation, payoff, replay potential, pacing, rhythm, no-sound understandability.
10. **Kids Shorts trend intelligence** — what is currently working in kids short-form content, observed from real uploads. A trend is recorded as a trend, with its date — it becomes a permanent rule only after verification (see below).

## Per-video record format

Every video analysed for the library uses the 25-field record format from `kids-animation-reference-analysis.md` (URL, channel, title, publication date, duration, aspect ratio, animation category, probable production method, character design, environment design, camera angles, shot sizes, camera movements, lens and perspective observations, lighting, animation and motion, facial performance, dialogue and voice style, sound design, editing rhythm, story structure, audience appeal, distinctive production techniques, techniques reusable in our system, technical limitations and uncertain observations), plus a timestamped shot breakdown where possible. Never fabricate a field. An uncertain observation is recorded as uncertain. In the records below, related fields are grouped on one line and every field number is shown, so all 25 are covered for each video.

## Selection rules

- **All production categories.** AI animation, traditional 3D CGI, 2D cartoon, game animation, hybrid production, stop-motion-inspired, and every other method the skill covers — the library learns the craft from every method, not from one.
- **Large channels AND small breakout channels.** Big channels show what works at scale; small breakout channels show what is new. Both are studied.
- **Recent 30 days prioritised, plus evergreen examples.** Recent uploads show what works now; evergreen examples show what keeps working. A record notes which of the two it is.
- **Shorts studied separately from long-form.** A channel's Shorts and its long-form uploads are different formats with different rules — they are recorded separately.

## How a discovery enters the library

1. A new reference link arrives (from the user, or from research). Inspect the actual video — the whole video, not just its thumbnail.
2. Compare with the library: what is already known, what is new, what is better than what exists.
3. Identify new techniques and improvements, classified into the ten areas above.
4. **Verify BEFORE making it a permanent rule.** A technique becomes a permanent rule only after it is observed, checked against the existing skills, and confirmed to work. Never treat every new trend as a permanent rule.
5. Before adding anything, check whether the knowledge already exists in another skill — extend that skill instead of duplicating it. Preserve all previous capabilities; never delete, replace, or weaken an existing skill.
6. Record the date and source of each discovery with the record itself.
7. When a verified technique belongs in a skill, update that skill (or the `kids-family-animation` skill) and keep the library record pointing at it.

## Verified techniques — cross-research patterns (2026-11-13)

These patterns were observed across multiple independent research streams. They are recorded here and in the skill (§17); a pattern is a verified observation, and it becomes a production rule only where the skill states it as one.

- **AI stills + slow movement** is the dominant small-channel method: photoreal or 3D-style AI still images, slow Ken Burns zooms/pans, narration over stills — NOT true motion animation (Dream AI BD, @smallaishorts, Ai Fable, Creature Capers, Laddoo Chor Milo). Camera in these is static or near-static; cuts do the work. Area 4.
- **Recurring hero + rescue/emotion-of-the-day** (Tamanna; Pitbull vs Chhotu) — one fixed character, a new crisis each video, emotional payoff, Part 2 or vote hook at the end. Areas 5, 6.
- **Multi-part sagas + Part 2 hooks** (@smallaishorts atomic bomb; Ai Fable; Creature Capers) — the ending is a promise, not a resolution. Areas 6, 9.
- **Game-engine animation is viral** — BeamNG.drive, Garry's Mod, Minecraft (23M, 24M, 2.1M views) — a production category the skill already lists (method 12). Area 3 (as observed detail).
- **Live-action costume-play Shorts are the biggest kids Shorts in this set** (774M, 596M, 515M) — music only, static camera, 10–30 s, one visual gag. Not animation, but the Shorts grammar (instant gag, static camera, music) matches the animation Shorts. Area 10 only.
- **Multi-language dubbing of one story** (@kidschatterbox, 7 languages) — a distribution technique, not a production technique; tiny audience so far. Area 7.
- **Static camera + hard cuts** appears in the 4-video watch, the channel research, and the Cocomelon video — the camera decision system in the skill notes that viral Shorts often use NO camera movement at all. Area 1.
- **AI-made labels** are common on small channels (Dream AI BD, Laddoo Chor Milo, Islamic dua short) — disclosure is visible in the wild. Area 10.
